This is the **reference** for a section's blocks; `CLAUDE.md` keeps only the mechanics of building
and running the suite, plus the rules that govern adding to it.

Read a section's entry here before you edit that section. `assertions=` in the summary line is the
verdict, and a block described here that stops running is a deleted guarantee.

### §11

§11 drives the real WHOOP export. It locates the CSV from `#filePath` rather than the working directory, and it derives its expected day keys from the parsed rows instead of hardcoding counts — the device time zone decides `startOfDay`, and two 23:30 fragments sit on a date boundary. ### §12

§12 is the day-selection guard.
